About
Actress. Writer. Director.
Built for the international stage.

Jasmine Métivier is a SAG-AFTRA actress, writer, and director based in New York. Her screen work spans U.S. premium cable, international streaming, and global commercial campaigns — with credits distributed across 52 countries grossing $2.4M at the box office.

Conservatory-trained under Susan Batson, Anthony Abeson, and the Actors Studio, she brings a rigorous technical foundation to character-driven, emotionally grounded performance. She was recently called back for the Sammy Davis Jr. biopic directed by Colman Domingo, who described her work as "wonderful and grounded."

She is the founder of Metivier Productions and holds an active India work visa — positioning her as one of very few New York-based actresses able to work simultaneously across U.S. and Indian productions.

The Hulls of
Wallabout

A supernatural thriller short set at the Brooklyn Navy Yard during America's 250th anniversary. Drawn from the untold history of the HMS Jersey prison ship and the 11,500 Revolutionary War soldiers who perished in Wallabout Bay.

Written, directed by, and starring Jasmine Métivier. Shot on location in Brooklyn. Currently in post-production.
